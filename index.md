# TradeRef Privacy Policy

*Last updated: June 2025*

TradeRef is an offline industrial reference application. This privacy policy explains simply and plainly how your information is handled.

## What we collect

Nothing. TradeRef does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device and does not require an account, login, or internet connection.

## Feedback

The app includes an optional feedback feature. If you choose to use it, your message is sent directly from your device's email client to officialtraderef@gmail.com. We do not see or store your email address unless you include it in your message. Feedback is entirely voluntary.

## Third parties

TradeRef does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Children

TradeRef does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a future version — for example if account features are added — this document will be updated and the new policy will be available before any such update is released.
